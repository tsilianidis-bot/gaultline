# FAULTLINE — Evidence Verification and Thesis Validation (pilot)
Status: research-only; no public claim or prediction authorized.
Created: 2026-10-14. This document records policy from this date forward; it is not proof of an earlier forecast.

## Purpose
Screen social-media and purported institutional trading claims before using them as market evidence. Independently evaluate prospective market theses without hindsight, fabrication, or unsupported crisis probabilities. Reuse existing Five Questions and claim-provenance mechanisms; do not add a navigation section.

## Evidence gate
Every claim record must retain: literal allegation (not asserted as fact); source URL or identified attachment; creator/resharer distinction; alleged subject and legal entity; event time versus report time versus first-observed time; extraction method; independently located primary documents; contradictory evidence; reviewer and decision; data freshness; and explicit limitations. Screenshot timestamps are NOT evidence of transaction time.

Statuses: `unverified`, `partially_corroborated`, `verified`, `refuted`, `satire_or_parody`, `indeterminate`. Treat satire as a content-origin classification; it cannot alone establish whether an underlying economic event occurred. A blue check, follower count, or AI-generated explanation is not proof. Verify UK ownership and registrations via FCA/Companies House, and fund positions via attributable fund filings, official statements, or defensible independent reporting. Distinguish UK-largest by AUM, domicile, and date.

**Hard gate:** Never treat `unverified`, `satire_or_parody`, `refuted`, or `indeterminate` items as corroborating market evidence; do not put them in risk scoring, trade alerts, crash probabilities, or investor recommendations. Missing evidence fails closed. Human-reviewed `verified` still describes only the narrowly verified proposition. Unverified claims may appear *only* in internal research, explicitly marked as allegations. Avoid repeating personal identity allegations publicly.

## Five Questions presentation (future, gated)
- NOW: independently documented market observations, timestamps, and freshness.
- WHY: primary evidence, alternative explanations, and noncausal caveats.
- OUTLOOK: conditional scenarios and invalidation; no event probabilities without validated calibration.
- WATCH: dated observable measures (credit spreads, breadth, liquidity, volatility, positioning) and prospective thresholds.
- ACT: conditional, nonpersonalized risk-management choices; never imperative "sell everything" instructions.

## Thesis ledger protocol
Each forecast hypothesis must record immutable original source/commit or archived timestamp, author, scope, baseline, horizon, event definition, predetermined success and failure criteria, disconfirming conditions, evidence datasets, revisions, and exact time data became known. Publication of a retrospective summary must clearly distinguish preexisting prediction from post-event analysis. No backdating. If the original thesis is only known from conversation recollection, mark the original date as **unverified** pending original timestamped artifact.

Keep an append-only evaluation log. Lock criteria **before** testing subsequent observations; if criteria are drafted after events, label the result retrospective/exploratory, not out-of-sample. Compare index baseline and simple naive rules; record misses and false alarms. Rate-condition Pressure Index v1 is not a validated crisis prediction model. Do not promote v2 shadow outputs or put crisis probabilities on public pages absent independent evaluation.

Next evidence collection: save canonical original URL and original publication timestamp; determine original creator versus reposter; resolve exact spoken statement; check FCA, Companies House, audited fund identity and AUM; obtain credible contemporaneous transaction confirmation; log contradictory sources. Do not present this as validation of the founder's crash thesis.

## Promotion acceptance checks
1. Missing source, transaction record, or legal entity remains unverified.
2. A parody watermark cannot upgrade an allegation to fact.
3. Reposts never change the original first-published time.
4. Later market selloffs cannot be used to backdate forecast criteria.
5. Historical case files are clearly marked retrospective where applicable.
6. No modifications to billing, migrations, deployment settings, Pressure Index, or public intelligence in this pilot.
7. A second reviewer signs off on verified institutional claims before external use.
