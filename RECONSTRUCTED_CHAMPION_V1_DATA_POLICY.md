# Reconstructed Champion V1 Historical Data Policy

## Status and Scope

This policy is locked **before** extended Champion V1 scoring. It creates two strictly separated research tiers. It does not alter `CHAMPION_V1_FROZEN_20260819`, live production scoring, the legacy 317-row history, V3-H status, or public claims.

> **Dataset labels are not interchangeable.** `VERIFIED_POINT_IN_TIME_HISTORY` may support only point-in-time research. `RECONSTRUCTED_HISTORICAL_RESEARCH_HISTORY` may answer retrospective model-behavior questions but cannot support claims that FAULTLINE actually observed, warned, or predicted a past event at the time.

## Tier A — VERIFIED_POINT_IN_TIME_HISTORY

| Policy field | Locked decision |
|---|---|
| Status | **BLOCKED — no complete common history** |
| Limiting input | `BAMLH0A0HYM2` has no accepted ALFRED vintage history and FRED now retains only the most recent three years. |
| Result | No additional Tier A score is generated by Phase 1B. The existing post-2023 `verifiedHistoricalScores` ledger remains separate and retains its `REVISED_HISTORICAL` quality label. |
| Prohibited claim | No score in this tier may be described as reconstructed point-in-time evidence before the credit-spread vintage gap is resolved through an authoritative source. |

## Tier B — RECONSTRUCTED_HISTORICAL_RESEARCH_HISTORY

| Policy field | Locked decision |
|---|---|
| Intended coverage | 2000-01 through the current completed monthly window, subject to explicit source gaps. |
| Formula | Exact frozen Champion V1 calculator; no changes to weights, thresholds, transforms, rounding, caps/floors, or static AI concentration baseline. |
| Monthly decision timestamp | Last U.S. business day of each calendar month, with the actual latest eligible observation date stored separately for every input. |
| Daily inputs | Use the last source observation dated on or before the monthly decision timestamp. |
| Monthly CPI/PPI/UNRATE/FEDFUNDS | Use the latest completed reference-month observation dated **no later than the preceding calendar month**, preserving a conservative release-lag rule. Calculate CPI/PPI year-over-year changes from the same revised source family and require 12 valid prior observations. |
| Missing data | No interpolation, no fallback constants, no carry-forward across a documented source gap, and no score if a required input is unavailable. Persist `INCOMPLETE` with missing flags. |
| Quality classification | Every reconstructed score is `REVISED_HISTORICAL` at the aggregate level. Per-input provenance preserves `ARCHIVED_OFFICIAL_REVISED`, `CURRENT_OFFICIAL_REVISED`, or `OFFICIAL_PROXY_RECONSTRUCTED` labels. |
| Scoring restrictions | Outcomes, legacy scores, requested crisis values, or later observations never enter score construction. |

## Explicitly Rejected Substitutions

`EFFR`, `FEDFUNDS`, BGCR, TGCR, and other rates are not silently inserted as SOFR. The only approved predecessor is the Federal Reserve Bank of New York primary-dealer Treasury general-collateral repo survey rate because the Federal Reserve’s own historical-proxy note documents its economic relationship and limits. It remains a separate, disclosed reconstruction input.

No alternative credit-spread index is approved. The reconstructed series uses only an archived copy of the **exact frozen** ICE BofA high-yield OAS series plus the currently retained exact FRED series. A correlated substitute would require a new policy version and is outside Phase 1B.

## Validation and Disclosure Rules

The reconstructed history may be used for retrospective tests of score range, relative stress discrimination, vector mechanics, rank behavior, calibration, and descriptive difference from the immutable legacy series. It may not be used for point-in-time predictive-performance claims. V3-H remains shadow-only and is excluded from all Phase 1B comparisons.

## Outcome Observation Selection Rule

The reconstructed outcome ledger is append-only. During its first load, an upstream long-range `range=max` request returned provider-downsampled monthly observations even though the requested interval was daily. Those observations remain in the ledger as audit evidence but are **not evaluable**. Every reported metric must select only rows with `sourceMetadataJson.dataCadence = DAILY_CONFIRMED_PERIOD_QUERY`, generated by a period-bounded Yahoo query that returned 6,716 daily bars for the target window. No earlier row may be updated, deleted, or silently replaced.
